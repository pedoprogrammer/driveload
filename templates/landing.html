What to expect when saving public media and accessible Drive files.

Which public video sites does DriveLoad support?

DriveLoad supports many public social and embedded-media pages. Availability depends on the website and excludes DRM-protected or access-restricted media.

What file types does DriveLoad support?

DriveLoad supports public videos from many websites and accessible Google Drive videos, PDFs, Word documents, presentations, spreadsheets, and images.

Is DriveLoad free to use?

Yes. The free plan includes 3 lifetime downloads with no credit card required. The Pro plan ($9.99/month) gives you unlimited downloads and priority support.

Are my Google cookies safe?

Google cookies are only needed for Drive files that require your authenticated access. You can clear saved cookies from the dashboard at any time.

Can DriveLoad bypass DRM or private access controls?

No. DriveLoad is for public, non-DRM media and files you are authorized to access. It does not bypass DRM, paywalls, or account permissions.
